The two products sell two different things, and they are never swapped for each other.

IMEI checks (Reselling.US). A check is paid in this order: your own Sickw key if you saved one, which is between you and Sickw and unlimited on every plan; then any checks your plan includes that month; then checks you bought from us, at the price Sickw charges us. A plan’s credits are given each calendar month and do not carry over. Credits bought in a pack do not expire while your account exists and are used after the month’s plan credits. An AI action that fails returns its credits. To keep the free tier available, we may limit how many free AI actions run each day across the Studio.

Checks and credits have no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and end when the account that holds them is closed.

A report is information for your own decision, not a guarantee that a device is clean or that you may lawfully buy or sell it.

AI output can be wrong: a product may not look exactly like the real one, and a result can resemble other images. Review every result before you publish it, and do not present an AI image as a photograph of a specific item you are offering when it is not.
