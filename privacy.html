Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026, updated 22 September 2026.

What stays in your browser

Your drafts, saved templates, brand kit (phone number, website, business name), your area (ZIP or city), the photos you upload and your export history are stored in your browser on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Your area is looked up in the browser against a list of US ZIP codes and cities that ships with the site. If you press “Locate me”, your browser asks your permission and the position is matched against the same list on your device; it is not sent to us.

What we store

If you create an account: your email, a salted password hash (or, if you sign in with Google, the email Google confirms), your plan and how many exports you have used this week or month. We do not store the ads you download.

When something leaves your device

  • Publishing to the community gallery. Only if you choose to share a background. It becomes public to other users of the studio.
  • AI backgrounds. If you generate a background, the words you type are reduced to a few keywords and sent, with our own prompt, to an image-generation provider (Google’s Gemini or fal.ai). Your account email is not sent to them.
  • A shop link. If a shop gives you a connect link (for example iPhones.LA’s Auto-post page), the ads you download are also sent to that shop’s picture library. Ads that show a phone number, website, QR code or watermark are not sent, and the studio tells you when that happens. The link only exists if you open one.
  • Fonts, code libraries and hosting. The site is hosted on Netlify. The drawing library comes from cdnjs (Cloudflare); the font picker loads fonts from Google Fonts; HEIC photo conversion and QR codes load a small library from jsDelivr or unpkg when you use them. Like any website request, these services see your IP address.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we keep only your Stripe customer reference so your subscription can be managed.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, run third-party advertising trackers, or read the ads you make.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, text (562) 999-4994 from the phone number you want us to reply to, with your account email. Community publishes can be removed on request the same way.

Contact

Privacy questions: text (562) 999-4994.
