Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026, updated 11 October 2026. BUYBACK.AD (“we”, “us”) runs Graphics Studio and is responsible for the personal data described here.

The short version. Most of what you make stays in your browser. We keep your account, your plan and your usage counts, and Stripe keeps your payment details. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or run ad trackers.

What stays in your browser

Your drafts, saved templates, brand kit (phone number, website, business name), your area (ZIP or city), the photos you upload and your export history are stored in your browser on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Your area is looked up in the browser against a list of US ZIP codes and cities that ships with the site. If you press “Locate me”, your browser asks your permission and the position is matched against the same list on your device; it is not sent to us.

What we store

If you create an account: your email, a salted password hash (or, if you sign in with Google, the email Google confirms and the name on that Google account), when the account was created, your plan, how many exports you have used this week or month, and, if you subscribe, your Stripe customer reference. We also keep your AI credits (how many of the month’s you have used, and how many you have bought), daily counters of how many backgrounds you published, and the references of the Stripe payments that bought credits, so the limits work and a payment is never counted twice. The AI backgrounds you generate are kept on our server, your newest 48, so the studio can load them for you; older ones are deleted. We do not store the ads you download.

When you buy a plan or credits we also keep the reference of your checkout, your subscription’s status, plan and renewal date, and a record that you agreed to the Terms of Service: the version and the time. The same record is kept with the payment at Stripe.

When something leaves your device

  • Publishing to the community gallery. Only if you choose to share a background. It becomes public to other users of the studio, with the part of your email before the @ shown as the author.
  • AI backgrounds. If you generate a background, the words you type are reduced to a few keywords and sent, with our own prompt and the choices you made (style, colours, size), to an image-generation provider (fal.ai or Google’s Gemini). Products you pick are sent as our own product photographs. Your account email is not sent to them.
  • A shop link. If a shop gives you a connect link (for example iPhones.LA’s Auto-post page), the ads you download are also sent to that shop’s picture library, with a list of the words and product pictures on the ad so the shop can write the listing. A phone number may be on the picture; ads that show a website, QR code, street address, social handle or watermark are not sent, and the studio tells you when that happens. The link only exists if you open one.
  • Printing and posting through SCANS.AD. Only for accounts that are also SCANS.AD members, and only when you choose “Order prints + posting”: the ad image, its name and size are handed to the SCANS.AD site in a new browser tab.
  • The video maker. Videos are made on your device and nothing is uploaded. It reads the area from your brand kit in this browser.
  • Fonts, code libraries and hosting. The site is hosted on Netlify. The drawing library comes from cdnjs (Cloudflare); the font picker and the site’s Look menu load fonts from Google Fonts; HEIC photo conversion and QR codes load a small library from jsDelivr or unpkg when you use them; if Google sign-in is offered, its button loads from accounts.google.com. Like any website request, these services see your IP address.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. The payment form at checkout is Stripe’s, shown inside our page: your card details go straight to Stripe and we never see or store your card number. Stripe also receives your email, and the billing name, address and tax ID you choose to give for your invoices. We keep only your Stripe customer reference and the records above, so your subscription can be managed. Stripe uses its own cookies in its payment form to prevent fraud; see Stripe’s privacy policy.

Why we use it

We use your data to provide the Service you signed up for (your account, your plan, your downloads and credits), to take payment and keep the records the law requires, to prevent abuse and keep the Service secure, and to answer you when you contact us. Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, our legal bases are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and securing the Service, and complying with legal obligations.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, run third-party advertising trackers, or read the ads you make. We do not use your content or your prompts to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

The studio uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to save your work on your device. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe’s payment form sets its own cookies for fraud prevention when you check out.

How long we keep it

  • Account records: while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete its record, its usage counts and its generated backgrounds.
  • Payment and tax records: Stripe keeps them, and we keep the references to them, for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Generated backgrounds: your newest 48; older ones are deleted as new ones are made.
  • Community publishes: until you or we remove them.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (PBKDF2), the site is served over HTTPS, sign-in uses signed tokens, and payment details are held by Stripe, not us. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Your plan, card and invoices can be managed yourself in Plan & billing, then Manage billing.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what the Service needs. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask to verify the request.

If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, text (562) 999-4994 from the phone number you want us to reply to, with your account email. Cancel any paid plan first in Manage billing. Community publishes can be removed on request the same way.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Where your data is

We and the services above process data in the United States. If you use the Service from elsewhere, your data is transferred to the United States.

Changes

We update this policy when what we do changes; the date at the top says when. For a change that matters, we will tell you in the Service or by email before it applies.
