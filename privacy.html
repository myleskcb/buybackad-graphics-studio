Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-12 · Reselling.US and BUYBACK.AD Graphics Studio · iPhones LA LLC (“we”, “us”) is responsible for the personal data described here.

The short version. One company makes both products, and this one policy covers both. We keep what each product needs to work: your account, your plan, and in Reselling.US the records you keep there. Most of what you make in the Studio stays in your browser. Stripe keeps your payment details. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, run ad trackers, or use your figures to price anything for anyone else.

What Reselling.US keeps

  • Your account: email, name, a hash of your password (never the password itself), when you confirmed your email and when you last signed in. A code we email you is kept only as a scrambled copy, for an hour.
  • Your shop: its name, time zone and settings, the contact details you choose to show buyers (phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, city), and your Sickw API key if you save one, stored encrypted.
  • What you record: devices, their IMEIs and serials, costs, prices, sales, expenses, receipts, invoices, notes, and the IMEI reports you run.
  • Photos you add, with the location and camera details taken out before they are kept.
  • On plans that have them: your buyers’ names, phone numbers and Instagram usernames, which you add and can delete; your team members’ emails; and, for your shop’s own site, the sell requests, orders and payments it takes and counts of its visits and quotes.
  • Sign-in attempts, kept for a day, to stop password guessing.
  • If you subscribe or buy checks: your Stripe customer reference, your subscription’s status, plan and renewal date, and the version of the Terms you agreed to and when.
  • If you ask us to build your website: your name, shop, email, phone if you give one, city and your answers, used only to write back about it.

What the Studio keeps, and what stays in your browser

  • In your browser only, never sent to us: your drafts, saved templates, brand kit (phone number, website, business name), your area (ZIP or city), the photos you upload and your export history. Your area is matched against a list of US ZIP codes and cities that ships with the site; if you press “Locate me”, your browser asks first and the position is matched on your device.
  • On our server, if you create an account: your email, a salted password hash (or, if you sign in with Google, the email Google confirms and the name on that Google account), when the account was created, your plan, how many downloads you have used this week or month, your AI credits used and bought, daily counters of the backgrounds you published, and your Stripe references, so the limits work and a payment is never counted twice.
  • The AI backgrounds you generate, your newest 48, so the Studio can load them for you; older ones are deleted. Ads you save to your library are kept until you remove them. We do not keep the ads you only download.
  • If you buy a plan or credits: your checkout reference, your subscription’s status, plan and renewal date, and the version of the Terms you agreed to and when.

When something leaves your account

  • Pages you share. Anyone you send a device’s link, your shop page, your quote site or your store sees that page: photos, price, details and how to reach you. Never its cost, your notes or its IMEI. Stop sharing and the page is gone.
  • IMEI checks. Sickw receives the IMEI or serial of each check you run.
  • Messages you send. A WhatsApp, text or Instagram message the app writes opens on your own phone; it goes through that app, not through us. Follow-up emails on your quotes and invoices go out only if you switch them on.
  • Google Sheets. If you give us a Google Sheets link to import or to read a price sheet, we read that sheet from Google.
  • AI backgrounds. In the Studio, the words you type are reduced to a few keywords and sent, with our own prompt and the choices you made (style, colours, size), to an image provider (fal.ai or Google’s Gemini). Products you pick are sent as our own product photographs. Your email is not sent to them.
  • The community gallery. Only if you choose to share a background. It becomes public to other users of the Studio, with the part of your email before the @ shown as the author.
  • A shop connection. If you open the Studio from a shop’s connect link, the shop’s name, phone number and area may fill in your brand kit, and the ads you download or save are also sent to that shop’s picture library with a list of the words and product pictures on them. Ads that show a website, QR code, street address, social handle or watermark are not sent, and the Studio tells you when that happens. The connection exists only if you open one.
  • Printing and posting through SCANS.AD. Only for Studio accounts that are also SCANS.AD members, and only when you choose “Order prints + posting”: the ad image, its name and size are handed to the SCANS.AD site in a new browser tab.
  • Reading listings near you. Where a plan includes it, public marketplace listings are read for your shop. What a listing says (its words, photos, price and place) may be shared between the shops that use it so each listing is read once; your offers, prices, lists and decisions never are.

The companies we use

These companies process data for us, only to run the Services:

  • Render (United States) hosts Reselling.US and its database: your account, shop and records.
  • Netlify (United States) hosts the Studio, runs its server code and stores its account records, saved ads and generated backgrounds.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of reselling.us and delivers some code libraries the Studio loads (cdnjs). Like any website request, it sees your IP address.
  • Stripe handles payments, subscriptions, invoices and receipts. It receives your email, payment details, and the billing name, address and tax ID you choose to give. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Stripe’s payment form sets its own cookies to prevent fraud.
  • Sickw answers IMEI and serial checks. It receives the IMEI or serial.
  • fal.ai and Google make AI images (keywords, your style and format choices, our product photographs; no email). Google also provides Google sign-in on the Studio if you use it, Google Sheets if you link a sheet, and Google Fonts.
  • jsDelivr and unpkg deliver code libraries the Studio loads when you use the features that need them. They see your IP address.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in codes, password resets, and the emails you switch on.

Why we use it

We use your data to provide the Services you signed up for, to take payment and keep the records the law requires, to prevent abuse and keep the Services secure, and to answer you when you contact us. Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, our legal bases are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and securing the Services, and complying with legal obligations.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, run third-party advertising trackers, or read the ads you make. We do not use your content, your records or your prompts to train AI models. Your costs, prices, sales and buyers are never shown to anyone else or used to price anything for anyone else, our own shop included.

Cookies and browser storage

Reselling.US uses one cookie to keep you signed in; the Studio keeps you signed in with your browser’s storage. Both keep small settings, like light or dark, in your browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account and shop records: while the account exists. Deleting an account deletes its records, photos, reports, usage counts and generated backgrounds.
  • Payment and tax records: Stripe keeps them, and we keep the references to them, as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Generated backgrounds: your newest 48. Community publishes: until you or we remove them.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, saved keys are encrypted, the sites are served over HTTPS, sign-in uses signed tokens, and payment details are held by Stripe, not us. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Reselling.US exports and deletes from Settings. Your plan, card and invoices can be managed yourself in Manage billing. California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what the Services need. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask to verify the request. If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting your data

Delete a Reselling.US account from Settings. To delete a Studio account, or both accounts at once, email support@reselling.us from your account email. Cancel any paid plan first in Manage billing. Community publishes can be removed on request the same way.

Children

The Services are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Where your data is

We and the companies above process data in the United States. If you use the Services from elsewhere, your data is transferred to the United States.

Changes

We update this policy when what we do changes; the version at the top says which one this is. For a change that matters, we will tell you in the product or by email before it applies.
