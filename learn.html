How it works, in five short videos

You buy phones, gold, cars or cards. Your ad is how a stranger finds you. Graphics Studio is where you make that ad: pick a design, type your number, post it. Each video below is under 35 seconds and reads with the sound off.

1. What this is

Somebody near you has an old phone in a drawer, and they will text whoever looks like a real business. This is the tool that makes that ad: 600+ designs ready to post, for 13 things you buy.

20 seconds

2. How to make an ad

Three steps, about a minute. Pick a look, type your number, download. One tap changes every colour, six sizes are built in, and any design becomes a 10-second video.

32 seconds. The screens are the studio itself.

3. Look like the real business

In a feed your ad is the size of a thumbnail. A number you can read at that size, every line checked for contrast, one clear offer: that is what gets the text instead of the post typed over a photo.

24 seconds

4. Asking an AI, or picking one that is ready

With an AI picture you describe it, wait, check every digit of your number and try again, paying per try. Here the designs are already made and your number is typed, not guessed. We use AI too, for the background only: it never writes your words.

33 seconds

5. What it costs

Free: 3 ads a week. Starter: $25 a month for 100 ads, 25 cents an ad. Pro: $60 a month for 500 ads, videos too, 12 cents an ad. Business: $100 a month, unlimited. No contract, and designing is free with no account.
