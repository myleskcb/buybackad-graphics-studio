About Graphics Studio

Graphics Studio is a web app for making “We Buy” ads. You pick a design, type your phone number and area, and download an image you can post on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist, Instagram or Nextdoor, or print as a flyer. The number goes on big enough to read as a feed thumbnail, because it is how the people who stop reach you. A video maker in the same site makes moving We Buy Phones ads.

Who it is for

People who buy from the public and resell: phone buyers and repair shops, gold and silver buyers, coin dealers, car buyers, card shops (Pokémon and sports cards) and buyers of diabetic test strips. The designs, the product pictures and the wording are written for those eight markets, not for general-purpose flyers.

How it works

  1. Pick a design from the gallery on the home page, or by what you buy: phones, gold, silver, coins, cars, test strips, Pokémon cards, sports cards.
  2. Type your details. Phone number, website, your ZIP or city (so the ad can name the towns around you) and up to four selling points. Easy Mode takes it from there; the advanced editor gives you layers, fonts, colours and your own photos.
  3. Download. Pick a size (square, story, flyer, wide or tall) and download a PNG.

Every card in the gallery is checked before it is shown: the headline, the number and the call to action are measured for contrast on the card’s own pixels, the number has to read at thumbnail size, a card uses one or two typefaces, and no card carries invented reviews, years in business or prices. A card that fails is held back.

Video ads

The video maker makes short moving ads for phone buyers: phones fly in and land, the words are on screen within a second, and your number lands by the third. It has fourteen Los Angeles looks, lines in English, Spanish or both, and square, 4:5, story and wide sizes. The MP4 is made in your browser, nothing is uploaded, it needs no account, and a video does not count as an export.

It runs in your web browser on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to install.

What it does not do

  • It does not post to Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist or Instagram for you. You download the image and post it yourself.
  • It does not check whether an ad is allowed where you post it, or whether your business needs a licence to buy what you buy. Those rules differ by platform, state and city.
  • It does not set your prices. Any price you type on an ad is yours to honour.

Your data

Your drafts, brand kit, area and uploaded photos stay in your browser. For your account we keep your email, a salted password hash (or the name Google gives us), your plan, your export count and a few daily counters. The privacy policy lists every case where something leaves your device, and the terms cover billing and what you may post.
