# Graphics refresh, 2026-09-22

Scope: the Graphics Studio only. The showcase library (the 971 cards the landing
page and the hero wall show), the fonts it renders with, the photo treatment, the
copy on the cards, the share image, and the checks that guard all of it. The
classic templates in `app.js` and the chrome were not reskinned (see "What is
left").

Every number here was measured by a script in `scripts/` against the real
renders. The before numbers are the library at `60d57c9`.

## What was generic, measured

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| display typefaces on the showcase | **54** (Special Elite on 129 cards; Pirata One 58, Squada One 55, Bungee 47, Creepster, Nosifer, Press Start 2P, Kaushan…) | **7**, all OFL, vendored |
| support typefaces | 3 (Libre Franklin, Manrope, Zilla Slab) | 1 (Schibsted Grotesk) |

| palettes | 51, generated names from one word pot ("Blue Ticket", "Blue Deal", "Blue Market", "Indigo Cash"); the biggest (79 cards) brown ink and dark orange, which the owner's brief rules out | **29**, designed in OKLCH, split-complementary or triadic, a brown guard on every dark colour |
| photo treatment | 720 cards: greyscale photo under a flat pastel veil (median veil 56%) — the "dusty teal, dusty pink" wall | **766 duotones** solved to the same endpoints (shadows in the palette ground, highlights in its support hue) |
| median colourfulness of live cards | 0.267 | 0.286 (mean over all 971: 0.287 → 0.295) |
| median luminance of live cards | 0.484 | 0.476 (unchanged by design) |

New faces, each mapped from the voice the card already had so a condensed card
stays condensed: **Sofia Sans Extra Condensed** (condensed/bold), **Unbounded**
(tech/wide), **Big Shoulders Display** (was the stencil), **Bricolage Grotesque**
(grotesque), **Gloock** (serif, blackletter, vintage, and typewriter on
gold/silver/coins), **Young Serif** (slab, typewriter elsewhere), **Tilt Warp**
(script, marker, comic, horror, rounded). Weights are snapped to what each file
ships (rule 20), italic is dropped where there is no italic file, and each new
line is sized in the studio so it is no wider and no taller than the old line
was, so no font change can create a new collision or clip. Where a wide face
would have shrunk a headline below 72% of its slot, it is set in the condensed
face instead (60 cards).

Live cards per final display face: Tilt Warp 158, Unbounded 130, Sofia Sans XC
130, Young Serif 130, Gloock 118, Big Shoulders 65, Bricolage 49.

## Self-validation

- Every audit above re-run on the final library (`.render/audit/*.log`).
- Contact sheets looked at, before and after: `.render/audit/before-1.png`,
  `.render/audit/after-sample.png`, `.render/audit/after-palettes.png` (one card
  per palette), and the worst-legibility sheets.
- Landing in headless Chrome at 390 and 1440 (`scripts/landing_check.mjs`): 0
  console errors, 0 page errors, 0 failed or 4xx requests, no horizontal
  overflow. Production before the refresh: 36 requests / 1.33 MB first load on
  a phone. The refresh adds nothing to the first load except a bigger
  `index.json` (53.5 KB → 69.4 KB gzipped: the new audit fields). The fonts load
  only when a card is opened.
- A card opened from the grid on a 390px phone lands in Easy Mode with the new
  faces served from `assets/fonts/` and no errors.
- Hero picks: 16 of the owner's 17 still show; `GRD-DU02-30` now fails the
  legibility check and is left out, and the wall fills its slot.

## Re-running it

The recipe is in `OPEN-ITEMS.md` §H. `refresh_showcase.mjs` reads the records
from git, so to reproduce this refresh run it with `REFRESH_FROM=60d57c9`; after
this commit `HEAD` already holds the refreshed library and must not be
refreshed twice.

## What is left, and why

- **154 cards are held back** because a headline, phone or CTA still measures
  under 3:1 after two repair rounds. Most are small tracked CTAs on scriptRetro,
  hudTech and trustSeal layouts over busy grounds, where no ink colour clears
  the target; the fix is a plate or a layout change, which is a design decision
  per layout, not a colour pass.
- **Unverifiable facts are still in the templates**: "SINCE 2015", "500+ DEALS
  CLOSED", "4.9★ 200+ LOCAL REVIEWS", and quoted testimonials with names and
  towns ("Tasha B. · Bellflower, CA") on the trustSeal and reviewProof layouts.
  A reseller who posts one unedited is publishing a review and a history that
  are not theirs. They read as placeholders to be edited, which is why they were
  not rewritten here, but the owner should decide.
- **Generic comparative headlines** ("WE BEAT THE COIN SHOP", "WE TOP MAIL-IN
  OFFERS", "THE PAWN SHOP OFFERED PENNIES?") name no company and were kept.
- **The classic templates** (the "Classics" filter and the studio's own
  carousel) were not reskinned; they are the 19-pass chain and still carry
  lines like "TOP DOLLAR PAID".
- **`tplbg-data.js`** (454 KB compressed, base64 photos for the classics) still
  loads on the landing, a third of the first load. Deferring it touches the
  script order in index.html.
- The fonts gallery (`lab/fonts.html`) does not list the seven new faces yet.
