# BUYBACK.AD: search, structured data, speed and look, 2026-11-18

The audit the owner asked to see before the changes ("Show me the audit before
the big changes"), as it was shown (the page
https://claude.ai/artifact/SCVK2eQw5Lmb9z8bGFWg5B, private to the owner), with
the owner's two answers and the numbers after. The work is DESIGN-LAW 146 and
OPEN-ITEMS §BK, on claude/zen-dijkstra-7b5a2e, built on pull request 14
(claude/ads-improvement-roadmap-7632c8, 97e85c54).

## The two decisions

1. **AI crawlers that train models**: the options were allow them, allow the
   words but not the ad pictures, or block them (AI search and answer crawlers
   allowed either way; blocking Google-Extended also keeps a site out of
   Gemini's grounding). The owner: "Allow everything".
2. **"Latest theming"**, read as the full ad's look (rules 137 to 141) on every
   public page in place of rule 98's paper and ink: Navy & Gold in dark (and on
   a device set to dark), Silver & Blue in light, the full ad's own face with
   any of the owner's pairs a URL away, the studio's editor left as it is. No
   objection; built that way.

Findings:

1. Every link preview was the August card (violet, product cut-outs, no ad),
   on the landing, the video maker and all 18 category pages; privacy, terms and
   the Look Book had none.
2. No title said what resellers type: none of the 24 had "flyer", "cash for",
   "buyback ad" or "video ad", and "cash for gold" was on no page.
3. The landing had three h1s (Easy Mode's and the plans page's live in the same
   HTML), no main landmark, and a footer that jumped from h2 to h4.
4. The landing said little a crawler could read: the hero wall, the gallery and
   the colour sets are drawn by script, the category pages were linked only from
   the footer, and on a phone the bar had no links at all.
5. Structured data had gaps: no Organization; monthly offers only; nothing on
   the video maker, the Look Book, privacy or terms; five videos with no
   VideoObject; shown steps with no HowTo.
6. The FAQ stopped at 13 questions and skipped sizes per platform, the
   watermark, platform rules, your own number and name, ownership, phones.
7. robots.txt had no AI policy, there was no llms.txt, and the sitemap was a
   month old and missed the Look Book.
8. buyback.ad, www.buyback.ad and studio.scans.ad still had no DNS, so the
   canonicals stay on the Netlify address.

## After

- Every public page: a unique title and description in the trade's words, a
  canonical, the full Open Graph and Twitter sets with a real full ad as the
  picture, one h1, alt on every image, in-page links to all 18 lines.
- Structured data: Organization, WebSite, WebApplication (9 offers from
  `PLANS` and `PACKS`), WebPage per page, BreadcrumbList, HowTo where the steps
  show, FAQPage (22 on the landing), VideoObject x5. No rating, review or count.
- robots.txt allows every crawler, AI search and training alike; the owner's
  pages are noindexed by header and meta and left crawlable; sitemap.xml lists
  the 25 public pages with their ad images; llms.txt and llms-full.txt.
- The look: the full ad's language on the landing, plans, FAQ, bar and foot,
  the information and category pages, the Look Book, the video maker's page and
  the 404, in light and dark.
- Checked by scripts/seo_check.mjs; the existing checks as in OPEN-ITEMS §BK.
