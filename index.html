FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Do I need any design skills?

No. Pick a template, type your phone number, download. Layout, type and colour are already done, to rules written for buyback ads. If you want full control, the advanced editor is one click away.

What can I make ads for?

Phones & devices, gold & jewelry, silver, rare coins, cars & trucks, diabetic supplies, Pokémon cards and sports cards, with copy written for each market and a real photo behind every design.

Can I make video ads?

Yes. Any template exports as an 8-second video for Reels, Stories and TikTok, with or without sound. It records right in your browser at 1080p: MP4 from Chrome, Edge and Safari, WebM from Firefox. A video uses one export, the same as an image.

Where do people post these?

Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist, Instagram, TikTok and Nextdoor, plus print. Square fits every feed, and the advanced editor makes a Story 9:16, a letter-size flyer or a wide 16:9 banner from the same design.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Upload any photo, iPhone HEIC included, then blur it, tint it, or generate a background with AI. The plates behind your text stay solid, so your words stay readable over any photo.

How do I cancel?

Open your account menu, then Plan & billing, then Manage billing. You can cancel in the billing portal and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
