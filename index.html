FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Do I need any design skills?

No. Pick a template, type your phone number, download. Every layout, font pairing and color already follows what converts for buyback ads. If you want control, the advanced editor is one click away.

What can I make ads for?

Eight buying categories: phones & devices, gold & jewelry, silver, rare coins, cars & trucks, diabetic supplies, Pokémon cards and sports cards, 160+ templates written with real closing copy for each market, every Designer Library layout shot on real photo backdrops.

Where do people post these?

Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist, Instagram, Nextdoor, plus print. Exports are square 1080×1080 (up to 2160 on Pro), the format every feed prefers.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes, upload any photo (HEIC from iPhone included), blur it, tint it, or generate a background with AI. Solid color blocks in templates are semi-transparent so your photo stays visible.

How do I cancel?

From the plans page, Manage billing opens your Stripe portal where you can cancel instantly. You keep Pro until the end of the billing period.
