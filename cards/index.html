FedEx Office, Staples and Office Depot OfficeMax all offer same-day business cards to pick up in the store if you order by about midday, and other print shops near you may too. Times and stock vary by store, so call ahead.

These open Google Maps with the search you see. Nothing about you is sent anywhere until you open one.

What to ask for

  • Standard business cards, 3.5 × 2 inches, full colour on both sides.
  • 14 pt or 16 pt card stock, matte or gloss. Matte is easier to write on.
  • Bring the print PDF on your phone or email it to the store. It already has the 1/8 inch bleed and the crop marks, so tell them to trim to the marks.
  • A first box of 100 to 250 is plenty to hand out at meet-ups and leave at shops.
